Objections & Rebuttals
Try at least 3 rebuttals before letting anyone off the phone. Same-day or next-day callbacks only.
💡 Rules of Rebuttals
  • Always lead with an affirmation — "I completely understand" / "That's a great question"
  • Never go head to head with the client — use QBS to get them to arrive at the answer themselves
  • Try at least 3 rebuttals before letting anyone off the phone
  • Same-day or next-day callbacks ONLY — never beyond 24 hours
"I don't want to close my credit cards." ▼
Look — you've already used up about 90% of your available credit, and with these accounts maxed out, you're making interest-only payments that never hit the principal. Once we pay off these cards you'll have a fresh start with all new credit cards. Right now these are basically acting like expensive debit cards. However, if there are any cards you absolutely need to hold onto, just let me know and I can remove them.

QBS: "How long have you been trying to pay these cards down? Over that time do you feel like you're making real progress? As of right now how are the credit cards helping you? Do you think it's fair to say these cards are hurting you more than they're helping you?"
"I don't want my credit score affected." ▼
Your credit is being affected right now by these accounts. Payment history has never really been your issue — it's that these accounts are all maxed out. Sure, initially you'll see a dip, but you already have your mortgage and your vehicles — the main things you need credit for. Aside from that it's just to acquire more debt, which you don't need. We're going to take care of these accounts and help you increase your cash flow and regain your financial freedom.

QBS: "Is there anything in the short term you specifically need to leverage your credit for? Did you know the majority of your credit score is based on debt, and by getting rid of your debt it would actually help you in key categories?"
"I don't want to tarnish my relationship with these creditors." ▼
Each of these creditors has a fixed amount they settle on every time — this is a normal process. You're not giving up — we're putting you in a position to pay these accounts off as soon as possible. These banks will be more than happy to lend to you again because that's how they make their money. We just want to give them a reason to do business with you again. Everyone benefits: you get a lower payment and become debt free, and creditors get a tax write-off and a lendable customer.
"I've done something like this before and it messed me up." ▼
What you did was a traditional debt settlement program — I'm not going to bash it because it works, it's just very lengthy. It's mostly geared toward people who don't care about their credit. The Hybrid Modification Plan gives you the positives of loan consolidation WITH the positives of traditional settlement — giving you the best of both worlds. And unlike others, Cordoba Legal Group/Beyond sends out POA letters right away and starts working immediately.

QBS: "Do you know the name of the company you worked with? What was the biggest issue with that program? Would it be crazy for us to at least look at an option that will help you pay down this debt AND lower your DTI AND free up cash flow?"
"How do I know this isn't a scam?" ▼
We're not here to pressure you — we're on a secure recorded line. I'm happy to provide our website so you can do your due diligence. Cordoba Legal Group is A-rated with the BBB, licensed with the FTC, 3+ decades of experience. Beyond is A-rated on the BBB with 5-star reviews on TrustPilot. ClearOne works with Bar Licensed Attorneys in 49 states. Once enrolled you'll have full dashboard access to track everything every step of the way.

GENERAL: I completely understand. Look — you've been paying on this high interest debt for quite some time and chances are you've been thinking about this every single month. This is not the market or the economy to be in this position. You must take action now.

SPOUSAL (USE WISELY): Chances are you and your spouse have been talking about this debt for quite some time. The more you wait, the worse it gets. Just look at a calendar — I have [7 days out] or [10 days out] available, which works better?

"I can negotiate with my creditors myself." ▼
The reality is you cannot — not effectively. As a single consumer making high-interest payments every month, you have zero leverage. Creditors will never modify for someone who's still paying because they're making money off you.

We work with these creditors daily with established agreements. They send out Limited Power of Attorney notices immediately — creditors cannot take legal action against you while that's in place. The default rate is over 40% — creditors will not hesitate against an individual without legal backing.
"I just wanted a loan to pay off the debts." ▼
Absolutely — everybody does. The first thing I do is try to get you a loan with the best rate possible. Unfortunately, due to the amount of debt and current utilization, you can't qualify for good financing right now. If you got a loan, it would be 40-50% interest — trading bad debt for even worse debt.

After 6 on-time payments, the program offers an early graduation loan — pre-approved to pay off the resolved balance in one shot. That puts you back in position to qualify for prime financing.
"Will my creditors call me?" ▼
Yes — at first they'll call for the first month or so. That's perfectly normal. Before they go through with the modification, they want to try to keep you on that high interest payment. So you'll simply tell them: "I'm working with a law firm — don't contact me by phone anymore, only contact me by email." And just like that it's taken care of.
"Is this a bankruptcy?" ▼
Absolutely not — this is far from bankruptcy. People actually use this process to AVOID going that route. Bankruptcy is like the nuclear bomb of debt — wipes it out but leaves radiation on your credit for 7-10 years, scaring creditors away. This is specifically for financially responsible people who want to get out of debt while maintaining their ability to leverage credit in the future.
"How do you know creditors will work with you?" ▼
The greatest thing about this program is they only work with creditors they have pre-established agreements with. Certain creditors don't settle — like Mariner Finance, Tower Loans, Enerbank — and for that reason we don't work with them.

The program is fully performance-based — they don't get paid until your accounts are settled. When they pool thousands of clients' accounts and bring creditors millions of dollars at once, creditors love working with them.
"How did you get my information?" ▼
That's a great question — you're usually referred over to us through the credit bureaus, your primary financial institution, or an application or denial within the past 24 months. We're reaching out to everyone in the country. When COVID hit, inflation went up, interest rates went up, prices went up — but income didn't follow. So we're reaching out to everyone to see if there's anything we can do. Now I see here... (move on)

⚖️ Right of Offset vs. Cross-Collateralization

RIGHT OF OFFSET

If a client enrolls a debt with the same bank they bank with, that bank can reach into their checking account and apply those funds to the debt — WITHOUT the client's permission. Advise client to change banks or keep a very low balance in that account.

CROSS-COLLATERALIZATION

When collateral used to secure one loan is also used to secure other loans with the same lender. If a client defaults on one, the lender can seize collateral for all. Always ask about loans and mortgages at the same bank as enrolled debts.
